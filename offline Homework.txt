how can i copy this whole conversation and save it locally

You can absolutely save this whole conversation locally; the exact steps depend on your device. Here are simple options:

1. Copy & paste into a file (quickest method)

On a computer (Windows, Mac, Linux)

    With your mouse, click and drag to highlight the entire conversation in your browser.
    Press:
        Ctrl + C (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + C (Mac) to copy.
    Open a text editor or document app, for example:
        Notepad, Notepad++, VS Code, Word, Google Docs (while you’re still online), etc.
    Press:
        Ctrl + V (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + V (Mac) to paste.
    Save the file:
        In a text editor: File → Save As…, choose a folder, and save as something like coursera_chat_regex_notes.txt or .docx.

On a phone or tablet

    Long‑press on some text in the conversation until you see selection handles.
    Drag the handles to select all the text you want.
    Tap Copy.
    Open a notes app (Apple Notes, Google Keep, Samsung Notes, etc.) or any text editor.
    Create a new note and Paste.
    The note will be available offline once saved.

2. Save as a PDF from your browser (nice for reading later)

On a computer

    In your browser, press Ctrl + P (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + P (Mac) to open the print dialog.
    In the Destination / Printer dropdown, choose Save as PDF or Microsoft Print to PDF.
    Adjust layout if needed (Portrait vs Landscape, margins).
    Click Save, choose a folder, and name the file (e.g., coursera_conversation_regex.pdf).

On a phone

    Use your browser’s Share or Print option:
        Tap the browser menu (⋮ or ☰).
        Tap Share → Print (or directly Print).
        Choose Save as PDF as the printer/destination.
        Save the PDF to your device.
